About FL Deal Finder

What it does. Collects the public online menus of Florida medical marijuana dispensaries and shows the cheapest in-stock way to get what you want. Ask for an amount (like “2 oz flower”) and it works out the cheapest mix of package sizes at a single store.

Coverage. Trulieve and MÜV menus. Curaleaf locations are listed but their menus aren't included yet. Stores using Dutchie or Jane menus (GrowHealthy, Jungle Boys, Green Dragon and others) aren't included.

Stock & prices. Only items the dispensary reports as in stock are shown, using menus refreshed several times a day. Things can sell out between refreshes, so confirm on the dispensary's site before you go. On Trulieve's site, select the same store first: it shows stock for whichever store you have selected there. Prices are menu prices before any loyalty or first-time discounts. Store hours are regular weekly hours; holidays may differ.

Privacy. No accounts and no tracking. Your ZIP and settings are stored only in your browser. “Near me” uses your location once to pick the nearest ZIP code; it isn't stored or shared, and shared links never include your ZIP.

Not affiliated with any dispensary. Not medical advice. Florida medical marijuana purchases require an active Medical Marijuana Use Registry card, and state purchase limits apply.

Prices and stock come from each dispensary's public online menu and can change at any time.
